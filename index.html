Modelling primary bibliographies

The case study of Carlo Emilio Gadda

The project

The subject of this study is primary bibliographies – that is repertories which collect all works, typically printed, by an author – and their representation in the digital environment. The case study, for the complexity and peculiar characteristics of his literary production, has been selected in Carlo Emilio Gadda’s primary bibliography, which will be presented in detail in the following chapters, with the aim to provide a model for representing an author’s primary bibliography, both by reusing existing ontologies and introducing, whenever necessary and appropriate, custom properties and classes needed to satisfy requirements not covered by the semantics of reused models.
