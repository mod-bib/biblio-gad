Case studies

Among the multiple possibilities offered by Gadda's bibliography, the collection of short stories L'Adalgisa and the collection of essays Le Meraviglie d'Italia, Gli anni, Verso la Certosa and Le Meraviglie d'Italia-Gli anni have been selected as meaningful case studies.

L'Adalgisa

The collection, published for the first time in 1944 by Le Monnier, is interesting both in terms of editorial history and variants across different editorial realizations and for the genetic relationships it entartains with other works by the author. Indeed, L'Adalgisa was published for in 1945 by the same publishing house, in a second edition which removed three titles from the collection: the opening piece Notte di luna and the two short-stories derived from the novel La cognizione del dolore, namely Strane dicerie contristano i Bertoloni and Navi approdano al Parapagàl. The edition published in 1955 by Einaudi within the collection I sogni e la folgore reintroduced the texts and was reproposed in the completed works directed by Dante Isella and published by Garzanti from 1988 to 1993.

As mentioned, from L'Adalgisa a network of relationships radiates outward to works Gadda had either drafted and left unfinished or already published in periodicals. Specifically, the already mentioned story which opens the collection Notte di luna, was written in a first draft in Racconto italiano di ignoto del novecento, attempt of novel which Gadda wrote in 1924 and left at a compositional stage, until Dante Isella published it in 1983, whereas Strane dicerie and Navi approdano has already been published as tratti of La cognizione del dolore in the magazine Letteratura in 1938. A conspicuous group of stories, then, comes from the unpublished novel Un fulmine sul 220, edited posthumously by Dante Isella in 2000.
